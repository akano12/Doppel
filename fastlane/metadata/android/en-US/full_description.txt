<p>Really simple to use privacy manager for Android 8.1 and later. Doppel is the maintained continuation of XPrivacyLua by M66B, which is not supported anymore.</p>

<p>Revoking Android permissions from apps often lets apps crash or malfunction. Doppel solves this by feeding apps fake data instead of real data.</p>

<p><b>Doppel is an Xposed module</b>: it needs a rooted device with an Xposed framework, like LSPosed with System Framework in its scope (see the README).</p>

<p>Features:</p>
<ul>
<li>Simple to use</li>
<li>Manage any user or system app</li>
<li>Fake values for all apps or per app</li>
<li>A log of what each app tried, with the real and the fake value</li>
<li>Extensible with your own hook definitions and definitions shared by others</li>
<li>Light and dark theme, accent colors, a pure black background and a choice of app icons</li>
<li>Multi-user support</li>
<li>Free and open source</li>
</ul>

<p>Restrictions:</p>
<ul>
<li>Determine activity</li>
<li>Get applications</li>
<li>Get calendars</li>
<li>Get call log</li>
<li>Get contacts</li>
<li>Get location</li>
<li>Get messages</li>
<li>Get sensors</li>
<li>Read account name</li>
<li>Read biometric sensors</li>
<li>Read battery</li>
<li>Read clipboard</li>
<li>Read debugging state</li>
<li>Read memory and storage</li>
<li>Read device settings</li>
<li>Read identifiers</li>
<li>Read notifications</li>
<li>Read network data</li>
<li>Read sync data</li>
<li>Read telephony data</li>
<li>Read time zone</li>
<li>Read device features</li>
<li>Read VPN state</li>
<li>Record audio</li>
<li>Record video</li>
<li>Run commands</li>
<li>Send messages</li>
<li>Use analytics</li>
<li>Use camera</li>
<li>Use tracking</li>
</ul>

<p>A restriction hides data (an empty list) or fakes it (an empty or fake value). The README describes what each restriction does.</p>
